## 怎么判定

- **规律**：一条用英文写的、不含游戏名的规则，精确到可以照着实现并说明要验证什么；并且适用于不止一款游戏，或是行业标准机制（21 点 natural 优先级、分 A、百家乐补牌表、佣金舍入、各种加注系统等）。
- **改记指纹**：内容只是某一款游戏自己的设计或调参，同类游戏的开发者不需要照着做；或者现有技能已在“规律”层面教过，剩下的只是该游戏的参数。
- 同一条规律下游戏专属的细节写进 `notes` 的 `fingerprintNote`，不丢。
- 估算词数 = 2 × 该技能全部规律陈述的词数（按空白分词，与 `scripts/validate.mjs` 的 `wordCount` 一致；一半是陈述，一半是验证要点与章节框架）。新技能均 ≤ 540；放入现有技能的两条均不超过其剩余字数，且没有压缩或删除现有原文。

## 全部规律

### `math-blackjack-rules`（新，7 条，估算 474 词）

- **P001**（2 条：m16#16、m15#5）Score aces as 11 unless that busts, else 1. Only an unsplit opening two-card 21 is a natural; other 21s are ordinary and auto-stand. A bust ends the hand. Verify soft-hard transitions.
- **P002**（2 条：m15#11、r02#6）Settle in order: player bust loses; dealer natural beats all but a player natural, which pushes; player natural pays its premium; dealer bust pays; then compare totals, equal pushing. Verify every pair.
- **P003**（3 条：m15#10、m16#23、r02#7）Dealer draws below 17; declare soft-17 behaviour (S17 stands, H17 draws) and whether it draws only while a non-bust, non-natural hand remains. Verify soft-17 fixtures and no extra draws in all-bust, natural and surrender layouts.
- **P004**（3 条：m16#18、r02#8、r02#9）Declare the peek policy: with a peek on Ace or ten upcards a dealer natural ends the round before paid actions; without one, added Double and Split stakes also lose to a later natural. Verify liability.
- **P005**（2 条：m16#15、m15#13）Natural premium (half profit) and insurance (half stake) must land on the wallet grid: require grid-even stakes, or floor each hand's return separately, never the aggregate. Verify odd-unit stakes against aggregate-floor counterexamples.
- **P006**（2 条：m15#3、r02#0）Per new shoe, reveal one burn card and discard extra cards equal to its hard value (ace 1, ten-value 10), or burn exactly one when declared. Verify composition, cursor and ledger counts before dealing.
- **P007**（3 条：r02#1、r02#2、m16#4）Never change shoes mid-hand: check the cut card or reshuffle threshold only between rounds, finish the current round, then reshuffle from the persistent stream and reset the cursor. Verify cuts reached during draws and stream parity.

### `math-blackjack-actions`（新，6 条，估算 410 词）

- **P008**（1 条：m16#17）Offer insurance only on a dealer Ace for half the opening stake, if affordable; it returns principal plus premium only on a dealer natural, else is lost. Verify unaffordable stakes and both settlements.
- **P009**（2 条：m15#7、m16#19）Double only an untouched non-natural two-card hand with an allowed total (split hands too): debit its full current stake, draw exactly one card, then force stand or bust. Verify funding, eligibility and aces.
- **P010**（3 条：m15#9、m16#20、r02#11）Split pairs (declare value-equal or exact rank) for an equal stake within funding and hand caps; seat the sibling beside the current hand; deal second cards in declared order. Verify pair test, order and caps.
- **P011**（1 条：r02#12）Split aces take exactly one card each and cannot draw further or Double, except eligible re-splits below the hand cap; at the cap, finish pending ace hands automatically. Verify cap transitions and re-split funding.
- **P012**（2 条：m16#21、m16#22）Look up pair, then soft, then hard total; each Double or Split cell names a draw-or-stand fallback when rules or funds forbid it. Require complete own-key custom charts, rejecting missing, inherited or altered cells. Verify lookup priority.
- **P013**（1 条：r02#15）Evaluate 21+3 only from the player's two opening cards plus the dealer upcard, in order: suited trips, straight flush, trips, straight, flush. A-2-3 and Q-K-A are straights, K-A-2 is not. Verify exclusive categories.

### `math-betting-progressions`（新，7 条，估算 482 词）

- **P021**（1 条：m16#0）Check stop lines before each hand against a declared denominator: absolute bankroll, or bankroll minus committed stake, versus profit and loss thresholds; a run may end with zero hands. Verify both at opening.
- **P022**（1 条：m16#1）When the next strategic stake exceeds bankroll or table limit, either stop or reset the progression to its opening plan and retry, rejecting unplayed spins if still impossible. Declare which; verify reset scope.
- **P023**（2 条：m16#6、m16#7）Martingale doubles per consecutive loss, resetting on a win or max step; Paroli doubles per consecutive win, resetting on a loss or capped step; a push freezes both. Verify caps and next stakes.
- **P024**（3 条：m16#8、m16#9、m16#10）Stake = base × unit(index): D'Alembert +1 on loss, −1 on win (minimum 1); Fibonacci +1 place on loss, −2 on win; 1-3-2-6 advances on win, resets on loss or final step; pushes freeze. Verify edge indices.
- **P025**（1 条：m16#11）Labouchère stakes first plus last (the sole unit if one remains); a win deletes both ends, a loss appends the stake; an empty line or table-limit reset restores the initial line. Verify one-item and reset cases.
- **P026**（1 条：m16#12）Oscar's Grind tracks cycle profit, stakes just enough to reach a one-unit goal (at least one chip), raises the unit only after a win in an incomplete cycle, and resets at the goal. Verify partial goals.
- **P027**（1 条：m16#13）Scale a fixed-ratio multi-spot spread as one unit to the largest affordable whole-chip plan under the table limit; never cap spots independently; reject the spin if the minimum spread cannot fit. Verify ratios.

## 文件

- `patterns.json`：规律、改记指纹、notes（fingerprintNote 与疑问）、skills。
- `validate-patterns.mjs`：校验脚本。运行 `node validate-patterns.mjs`：307 个 id 各出现一次、每条规律至少一个成员、陈述 ≤ 40 词且为英文、不含 game-names.txt 里的名字（整词、不区分大小写；另有“名字拆词短语”提示）、新技能估算 ≤ 540、现有技能估算 ≤ 剩余字数、每条 U 条目有疑问。
- `build-patterns.mjs`：规律与指纹定义的数据源（`P(...)` 与 `F(...)` 行）；改动后先运行它，再运行校验脚本。
- `make-summary.mjs`：生成本摘要。
