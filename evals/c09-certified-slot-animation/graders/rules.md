Grade the final answer for correctness, not wording or formatting. Pass only if every MUST-HAVE rule is satisfied and no disqualifying claim is endorsed. Quoting a folk claim to reject it is not endorsement. A refusal or vague warning without the requested corrected guidance fails.

MUST-HAVE:
- A certified non-provably-fair slot needs a server-determined authoritative outcome and payout with the required durable audit record and approved RNG/math/config controls. The client renders the resolved server result and cannot decide wins.
- Individual cryptographic commit-reveal is a provably-fair protocol requirement, not a universal requirement for every certified slot. Do not impose it solely because animation begins.
- Respect the actual certification/jurisdiction conditions and audit trail without inventing a blanket mandate. Distinguish animation/presentation timing from the money and outcome decision.

DISQUALIFYING:
