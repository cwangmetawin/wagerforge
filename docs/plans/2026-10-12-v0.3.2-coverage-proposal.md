## Proposal

1. **Fix the denominator by triage.** Every mechanism in `mss-gaps.json` is triaged into one of three classes:
   - **teach**: a rule that a developer of a similar game needs. Examples are standard table-game rules, slot feature mechanics, and money and settlement rules.
   - **fingerprint**: one game's own simulation or tuning, such as snake movement, football AI, FPS weapons or race stamina. It is documented with its evidence in a reference file, not in skill text.
   - **drop**: a duplicate, configuration, or presentation entry recorded as a mechanism.

   Codex triages and Opus reviews the result. Boss and the owner approve it. The approved list becomes `docs/coverage/mss-mechanisms.json`. A family is then full when all of its teach items are taught.
2. **New skills.** The names are provisional. Each has at most 540 words, a router row and at least one eval case.
   - `math-blackjack-rules`: soft aces, S17 and H17, naturals and premiums, peek and no-peek, insurance, double, split and re-split, split aces, and dealing order from a shared or own shoe.
   - `math-baccarat-roulette-bets`: baccarat points and the third-card tableau, banker commission rounding, roulette call-bet expansion, pair and 21+3 side bets, and per-chip settlement rounding.
   - `math-betting-progressions`: Martingale, Paroli, D'Alembert, Fibonacci, Labouchère, Oscar's Grind, pattern bets, table-limit stop and reset, and the denominators of stop-loss and take-profit.
   - `build-live-table-operations`: betting windows and chip undo, per-chip and per-player limits, side bets that depend on a main bet, action deadlines, and seat scheduling.
   - `math-reel-modification-features`: reel insertion and shift, wild reels, coin-board height growth, injected symbols, and triggers from retained scatters.
   - `math-forced-outcome-shaping`: rejection sampling for minimum pay, forced-dead and forced-stop decisions, bounded fallbacks, and what each does to RTP.
   - Possibly one or two more after triage. Candidates are collector features and realtime authority.
3. **Fingerprint reference.** The per-game simulation rules, with their evidence, go in one reference that the QA skills point to. They are recorded without making skills longer.

## Effort

- Triage: one Codex ultra job (about 45 minutes) and an Opus review (about one hour).
- Skills: six to eight Codex ultra jobs in parallel (about one hour) and an Opus review of every diff (about two hours).
- Checks: a blind check and two reviews (about 1.5 hours). Evals take about 2.5 hours of wall time.
- In total, about one working day. Claude's share is mostly review.

## Risks

- Table-game rules, progressions and settlement rules are money rules. They are reviewed under criterion 4. No reference script change is planned.
- Codex discovery may dilute with about 75 skills. The expected-skill share is measured again.
