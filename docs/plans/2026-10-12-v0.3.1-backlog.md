## Criterion 4: what "full" means (written 2026-10-12 22:06)

The first independent check (brief of 21:45) used one sentence: a family is full when every mechanism its games use is specifically taught by a skill. It judged 3 of 27 families full. From 22:04 the briefs use the wording below, which spells that sentence out in line with the dashboard legend; it was first used for the gap analysis and is written here before the second measurement. The independent check receives only this definition, never the result we hope for. The sign-off report gives both verdicts, the first check's and the one under this definition, and names every family that is full only under this wording, with the reason.

- A mechanism is a rule that changes money, RTP, the outcome distribution, state transitions or settlement in a game: for example how a wild moves, what a collector collects, when a multiplier applies, how a feature ends. Configuration values (paytables, weights, tier counts, ladder values) and presentation are not mechanisms.
- A mechanism is specifically taught when some skill states the rule clearly enough that a developer implementing that game would get the money and the distribution right, and says what to verify.
- A family is full when every mechanism used by any of its games is specifically taught. Otherwise it is partial.
- Every untaught mechanism is classified as reusable (used by two or more MSS games, or a standard industry mechanic) or single-game (a bespoke rule of one game). A family left partial states why each remaining gap is single-game.
