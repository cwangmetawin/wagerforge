# fair-shoe-draw-ledger

> **Core insight:** Matching cards are insufficient: the player's retained epoch evidence must also fix preceding draws, legal draw decisions, composition transitions and the reveal boundary.

## When to use / When NOT
- Use when: cards persist across hands, rounds or run chunks, or multiple players share a stream.
- NOT for: basic RNG primitives (`fair-rng-core`) or authority-signature authentication (`build-signed-realtime-settlement`).

## Process
1. Version the contract: shoe/run/round epoch, rules/configuration, stream derivation, shuffle versus composition sampling, limits, rejection cursor and reveal scope. Composition lifetime may span several RNG epochs. Choose by dependencies, never by a multiplayer flag. MetaWin's production server (MSS) `games/baccarat-live/engine.ts` `Shoe.draw` samples remaining counts; MSS `games/blackjack-live/index.ts` `proveFairness` instead declares shuffle swaps.
2. Apply `fair-verify` epoch checks using evidence the player retained: commitment saved before the first bet, client seed generated by the player after commitment, accepted-bet receipts (including later refunds/voids) and contiguous nonces starting at zero. Allocate nonces only after stake debit; pre-debit rejections consume none. Separate bet nonces from shared-stream cursors. Missing commitment, seed, history or prior composition means **UNVERIFIABLE**, never PASS; a server-supplied replacement is insufficient.
3. Persist each draw's epoch, order, limit, role, rule/action context and composition transition; include burns and anonymous preceding runs. Preserve the caller's private run indices. Require nonnegative integer counts, permitted deck multiplicities, exact card conservation and previous closing composition equal to next opening composition. MSS `games/blackjack-table-live/drawLayout.ts` `recordDraw` and `publishDrawLayout` illustrate public limits/counts plus private `myRuns`.
4. Independently reproduce the reduction and every rejection-cursor advance; replay legal player actions, timeout actions, dealer rules and tableau. Reject extra draws, skipped cards, reused indices and validly sampled cards drawn illegally. A supplied draw count is not authorization. MSS `games/baccarat-live/engine.ts` `dealHand` determines legal third cards; MSS `games/blackjack-table-live/index.ts` `proveFairness` replays a caller-declared count, so adapter parity alone proves less.
5. Reveal RNG material only after every dependent future outcome retires. Never expose undealt cards or hole cards before their permitted reveal through proof, replay, reconnect or broadcast APIs. Round-scoped composition sampling avoids retaining future order under a revealed round key; MSS `games/baccarat-live/engine.ts` `Shoe` demonstrates this pattern.
6. Bound encoded input, declared draws, rejection work and response size. Preserve complete evidence through compact ledgers or authenticated pagination; truncation yields UNVERIFIABLE. MSS `games/blackjack-table-live/index.ts` `proofPlan` limits replay plans; parameterize budgets rather than copying platform limits.

## Correctness constraints
- **C7:** Plain SHA-256 seed commitments are valid; keyed derivation uses HMAC, not secret-prefix SHA.
- **C8:** Quantify reduction bias for the actual source and range; rejection-sample where needed. Power-of-two reduction is unbiased only for a compatible uniform source.
- **C11:** Pin hash/key encoding and reduction; full uint32 output does not make every range unbiased.

## Pitfalls / red flags
- Trusting declared counts hides illegal draws; accepting a fresh mid-shoe composition hides skipped cards.
- Revealing a shuffle key while its shoe remains playable exposes future cards.

## Verification
- Compare real play/tick and proof limits, order, cards, compositions and rejection cursors.
- Negative fixtures: illegal extra Hit/third card, omitted burn, skipped/reordered/repeated draw, forged composition, missing player evidence, oversized proof and premature future/hole-card disclosure.
